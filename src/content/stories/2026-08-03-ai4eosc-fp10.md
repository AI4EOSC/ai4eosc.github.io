The AI4EOSC initiative is proud to endorse the position paper "[The EOSC
Association calls for a distinct Work Programme-based Partnership for EOSC in
FP10 to accelerate excellent, scalable science in
Europe](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20485703) adopted by the [EOSC
Association (EOSC-A)](https://eosc.eu) at its most recent General Assembly,
held on May 28 and 29, 2026, in Brussels. In this way, AI4EOSC reaffirms its
strong commitment to the future of the EOSC and the expansion of the EOSC
federation, which requires, among other aspects, establishing a dedicated Work
Program within the EU’s 10th Framework Program for Research and Innovation
(FP10), enabling the consolidation of the developments carried out by the
[INFRAEOSC](https://eosc.eu/horizon-europe-projects) projects.

In addition, the AI4EOSC initiative is also aligned with the strategic vision
set out in the updated SRIA, SRIA 2.0, which establishes the following five
priorities to strengthen the EOSC in its role as the recognized Common European
Data Space for Research and Innovation: (1) operationalization of the EOSC
federation; (2) data sovereignty and quality of research data; (3) AI and EOSC;
(4) user-centricity and (5) strategic alignment and global collaboration.

AI4EOSC considers the EOSC a key enabler for trustworthy, ethical, and
sovereign AI in the EU. Being the place for FAIR and high quality data, the
EOSC is the natural environment to foster AI in science, through interoperable
and reusable data, models, and services, while also supporting reproducibility
in AI and data analysis.

Our collaboration contributes to this area, delivering an easy to use platform
for the development and sharing of AI models. In addition, the AI4EOSC
ecosystem will be expanded with new capabilities aligned with this strategic
area through the new projects under the collaboration umbrella
[FLUID-AI](https://fluid.ai4eosc.eu) and
[EOSC-ARENA](https://arena.ai4eosc.eu). Specifically, progress will be made in
generative AI (GenAI) with European sovereignty and ethical safeguards with
focus in science (via EOSC-ARENA), and new layers of data and models
interoperability will be added to ensure data accessibility and liquidity
across the entire life-cycle (FLUID-AI).

More details about the EOSC-A position can be found [here](https://eosc.eu/news/eosc-a-membership-unites-around-a-clear-position-for-the-future-of-eosc).
